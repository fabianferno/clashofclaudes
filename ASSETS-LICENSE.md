## What counts as art

- `public/assets/village/`: the painted village (builders, buildings, tents, scenery, UI pieces)
- `public/icon.png`, `public/logo.png`, `src-tauri/icons/`: the app icon and logo
- `docs/brand/`: the icon artwork, its source and the screenshot
- `site/public/img/`, `site/public/reel/`, `site/public/video/`, and the site's icons and social
  image (`site/public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `icon-256.png`, `og.png`)

Not art for this purpose: the code that draws the village (`src/village/`, including the procedural
art in `src/village/art/`, which is GPL like the rest of the code), and the fonts, which keep their
own licenses (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).

## What you may do with it

- Build, run, test and modify Clash of Claudes with the art in place, for yourself or inside your
  organization.
- Share unmodified copies of this repository, and contribute changes back to it.
- Show the art when you write about, review or make videos of Clash of Claudes.

## What needs permission

Anything else, in particular: **distributing a fork or a modified build to others with this art in
it**, or using the art in any other project, product or service. A fork you distribute must replace
the art with its own. The app falls back to its procedural art when the painted files are missing,
so a fork still runs without them.

The name "Clash of Claudes" and the icon are not licensed for use by forks either: a distributed fork
needs its own name and icon.

To ask for permission: https://x.com/fabianferno
